Upheld

A private prayer app for small groups. Keep your prayer lists in one place, pray through them together, and never let a request get lost.

Add Upheld to your phone

Exodus 17:12 (KJV)

Nobody should have to hold their arms up alone

Upheld started with a small prayer group that kept its requests on a slide deck that grew longer every week. Upheld gives a group one shared list that stays current, a simple way to pray through it, and a place to celebrate the prayers God answers.

Prayer time on a timer

Pick how long you have and which lists to pray through. Upheld shuffles them into one stack and shows one prayer at a time until the timer ends.

Request a prayer

Say who needs prayer and what’s going on. Upheld drafts a prayer you can edit before you send it to your group.

Private groups with approvers

Every group is invite-only. Approvers welcome new members and review new requests before the group sees them.

Answered prayer

When God answers, an approver moves the prayer to Praise. It leaves prayer time, and the group can look back on it.

Add Upheld to your phone

Prayer lists open only in the app, not in a web browser. Add it to your home screen, open it from there, and sign in to join your group.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open Upheld from your home screen.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots).
  3. Tap Install app.
  4. Open Upheld from your home screen.

Your group’s requests stay in your group

Every group is private. New members are approved before they can see anything, and prayers are only shown to signed-in members of that group.
